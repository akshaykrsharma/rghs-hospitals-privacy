Privacy Policy for RGHS Hospitals

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Important: RGHS Hospitals is an independent informational application and is not an official application of the Government of Rajasthan or Rajasthan Government Health Scheme (RGHS).

This Privacy Policy explains how RGHS Hospitals ("the App") handles information when you use the application. We aim to keep the App simple and useful while respecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following information:

  • Device and app information: Basic technical information may be processed by third-party services used by the App, such as advertising or analytics services, where applicable.
  • Approximate or precise location: If you grant location permission, the App may use your device location to help provide location-related features such as finding relevant hospitals or opening directions. The App does not need your location to browse hospital information.
  • Phone/call information: When you choose to call a hospital, the App may open your device's phone application with the hospital's publicly listed telephone number. The App does not itself collect or store your phone conversations.

2. Hospital Information

The App displays hospital information such as hospital names, addresses, telephone numbers, specialties and other publicly available or supplied information. This information may change over time. Users should confirm current RGHS empanelment status, services, availability and other important details directly with the hospital or an appropriate official source.

3. Location and Maps

Location access is optional unless a particular feature requires it. If you allow location access, it may be used to support location-based functionality. When you request directions, the App may open a third-party maps application or service. That third party may process your information according to its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in the App, we may use Google AdMob or another advertising provider. Advertising providers may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location and advertising-related information in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

For Google AdMob information, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide functionality, advertising, maps, hosting, analytics or other technical services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the policies of any third-party service you use through the App.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate features of the App, provide advertising, prevent fraud, maintain security, or comply with applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Information processed directly by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of identifying them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. Your Choices

You can control permissions such as location access through your device settings. You may also stop using the App or uninstall it at any time. Advertising choices may also be available through your device settings or applicable consent tools.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
